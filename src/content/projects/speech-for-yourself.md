## The challenge
Speech For Yourself needed a professional web presence that could do real work — not just look good, but actively help clients find the practice, understand what services are offered, and book an appointment without friction.

## What we built
- Designed and built a clean, accessible website from the ground up
- Built a custom client intake form for new patient inquiries
- Integrated Zanda Health for seamless appointment booking
- Optimized for accessibility, achieving a perfect 100 Lighthouse accessibility score

## Outcome
Speech For Yourself launched with a professional, fully functional web presence that works for both the practice and their clients — making it easy to connect, inquire, and book from any device.
