## The challenge

Lumen's previous site was a template that forced them into layouts that didn't honor their photography. Updates required filing a ticket with a freelancer. They wanted autonomy.

## What we did

- Designed three custom gallery layouts tuned for their most common shoot types.
- Built an Astro + headless CMS setup with instant draft previews.
- Wrote a short editors' handbook with screenshots of every common task.

## Outcome

The studio now publishes 3–4 new shoots per month on their own, and the site has become their best source of new client inquiries.
