## The challenge

Helio was spending heavily on paid ads with diminishing returns. Organic traffic was a rounding error, and their content strategy was "post whenever someone has time."

## What we did

- Full technical audit: crawlability, schema, Core Web Vitals, internal linking.
- Built a topical map around residential solar + financing + incentives.
- Produced a content calendar and wrote 24 long-form articles over six months.
- Instrumented GA4 + server-side events to attribute form fills to specific content.

## Outcome

Organic search now drives the majority of Helio's inbound pipeline. Their paid budget has been cut in half and redirected to retargeting the readers they earn for free.
