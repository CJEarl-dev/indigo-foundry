## The challenge
Oceanview Therapy had an existing WordPress site that needed to grow alongside the practice — new content, new states, new capabilities — without losing what was already working.

## What we did
- Restructured navigation and reorganized site content for clarity
- Added and removed pages to reflect current services and focus
- Built a dynamic popup announcement for new licensing states
- Launched the Lighthouse Spotlight blog, including the first published post
- Updated FAQ and ongoing content as the practice evolves

## Outcome
Oceanview now has a web presence that keeps pace with a practice serving clients across 8 states and growing — with a content foundation that the team can build on over time.
